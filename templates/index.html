How it works
  1. Pick the email provider to send through, and the supplier's type (Chinese / Non-Chinese) — SendCloud, EngageLab and Alibaba all support both. SendCloud routes Chinese suppliers through its Hong Kong region; Alibaba sends both supplier types through its Hong Kong server.
  2. Filling in the supplier and product creates the conversation as a draft and assigns its reference — before anything is sent.
  3. The RFQ goes out with the subject [RFQ - id] - … and its own Message-ID; the draft becomes Open.
  4. A reply is matched by In-Reply-To/References, falling back to the [RFQ - id] subject prefix — so keeping the subject intact is all a supplier has to do. The conversation then becomes Closed.
  5. Replies reach us at /webhooks/rfq/inbound, except Alibaba's, which are polled over IMAP.
  6. View the full conversation thread on the Tracking page.
